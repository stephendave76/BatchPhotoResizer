FAQ

Good to know

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser, so your photos never leave your device.

How many photos can I add?

There is no fixed limit. It depends on your device memory. Around 50 to 100 phone photos works well on a laptop. For more, process them in smaller batches.

Does the watermark land in the same place on every photo?

Yes. The position is saved as a percentage, so it sits in the same relative spot even when photos have different sizes.

Which format should I choose?

JPG is smallest for normal photos. PNG keeps transparency. WEBP is small and keeps good quality.

The collage looks cropped. How do I fix that?

Set Photo fit to "Fit (no crop)" or choose a cell shape closer to your photos.
